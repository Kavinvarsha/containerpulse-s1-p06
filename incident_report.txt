=== INCIDENT REPORT ===
## Incident Report: Elevated Anomalies and Rising CPU Utilization

**Date:** 2023-10-27
**Prepared By:** SRE Team

---

### Executive Summary
Significant anomaly rates have been observed in `pipeline-worker` and `web-service` pods, indicating potential resource saturation and instability. This is compounded by a rising CPU trend across critical services, suggesting an imminent capacity challenge.

### Affected Containers
*   **pipeline-worker-86d677d797-qvxs9**: 6.25% anomaly rate, rising CPU trend.
*   **web-service-854b589484-v69wq**: 6.15% anomaly rate, rising CPU trend.
*   **api-service-66cbf7988d-dndhp**: Stable anomaly rate (1.56%), but exhibits a rising CPU trend.

### Risk Level
**High**

The high anomaly rates (over 3x baseline) in key services, combined with system-wide rising CPU forecast trends for all affected pods, indicate an increased risk of throttling, Out-Of-Memory (OOM) kills, service degradation, and potential outages if left unaddressed.

### Root Cause Analysis
The primary cause of the observed anomalies is likely resource exhaustion, specifically CPU and Memory spikes, leading to throttling or OOM kills in the `pipeline-worker` and `web-service` pods. These services often handle bursty workloads, and current resource allocations (requests/limits) appear insufficient to accommodate peak demands. Additionally, some anomalies may be attributed to `Restart/CrashLoopBackOff` events, potentially triggered by runtime code failures or recent deployments. The overarching rising CPU trend across all services suggests an increasing overall load on the system, which will exacerbate existing resource constraints.

### Recommended Actions
1.  **Immediate Investigation:**
    *   Examine specific `pipeline-worker-86d677d797-qvxs9` and `web-service-854b589484-v69wq` pod events and logs for OOM kills, throttling messages, and crash loop indicators.
    *   Correlate `Restart/CrashLoopBackOff` events with recent image deployments or configuration changes.
2.  **Resource Optimization (Short-Term):**
    *   Adjust CPU and Memory requests/limits for the `pipeline-worker` and `web-service` deployments to better accommodate bursty workloads and prevent throttling/OOM kills.
    *   Consider implementing or tuning Horizontal Pod Autoscaling (HPA) for these deployments to dynamically scale with demand.
3.  **Capacity Planning (Medium-Term):**
    *   Analyze the overall cluster CPU utilization and compare against forecasted trends for all critical services, including `api-service`, to proactively address potential cluster-level resource saturation.
    *   Identify potential application-level optimizations for resource efficiency in `pipeline-worker` and `web-service` workloads.
4.  **Proactive Monitoring (Long-Term):**
    *   Enhance alerts for resource saturation (CPU/Memory utilization) and anomaly rates, especially for critical services, to provide earlier warning of emerging issues.
    *   Implement regular reviews of CPU forecast trends to inform capacity planning and resource allocation strategies.
